# Campbell Article Source Notes

## Is Sustainability Consulting Worth the Investment?

- **CDP, 2026:** [Extreme Weather Risk is Reshaping the Global Economy](https://www.cdp.net/en/press-releases/extreme-weather-risk-is-reshaping-the-global-economy). The published summary reports that companies anticipate US$714 billion in future financial impacts, principally from flooding, cyclones, and heavy rain.
- **Wang et al., Nature, 2024:** [Global supply chains amplify economic costs of future extreme weather extremes](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07147-z). Supplementary public summaries from [King's College London](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/news/climate-disruption-to-global-supply-chains-could-lead-to-25-trillion-net-losses-by-mid-century) describe global supply-chain loss scenarios through 2060.
- **Verdantix:** [Sustainability consulting market forecast](https://www.verdantix.com/venture/report/market-size-and-forecast--sustainability-consulting-2024-2030-global). The current public forecast differs from the supplied draft's historical numbers, stating a market above US$12 billion in 2024 and near US$30 billion by 2030.

## Publication treatment

The article will preserve the decision-focused argument but avoid unsupported individual project, engagement-fee, pricing, and savings claims. External statistics should either be attributed with direct URLs in the article's sources section or stated qualitatively where the draft's time period differs from currently verifiable reporting.
