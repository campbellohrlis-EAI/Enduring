# Verified Sources: Supply-Chain Article

## CDP: Extreme Weather Risk Is Reshaping the Global Economy

Source: https://www.cdp.net/en/press-releases/extreme-weather-risk-is-reshaping-the-global-economy

Published 12 May 2026. CDP states that 11,261 companies disclosing full environmental data through its 2025 questionnaire anticipated US$714 billion in future financial impacts from extreme weather; US$326 billion of this was expected from reduced production capacity. It reports that 48% of identified risks were expected within two years. The same release cites CDP’s 2025 Disclosure Dividend: median risk cost per company of US$39.4 million compared with US$3.1 million to mitigate, nearly 13 times lower.

## King’s College London: Climate Disruption to Global Supply Chains

Source: https://www.kcl.ac.uk/news/climate-disruption-to-global-supply-chains-could-lead-to-25-trillion-net-losses-by-mid-century

Published 13 March 2024. The university summary of Wang et al., *Nature* (2024), reports projected net economic losses of US$3.75 trillion to US$24.7 trillion in adjusted 2020 dollars by 2060 due to climate-disrupted global supply chains, depending on emissions pathway. It links to the paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07147-z.

## Publication Editing Guardrails

Use the figures only as system-level context, not forecasts for an individual company. Remove or soften unsupported claims about supplier questionnaire response rates, a particular reduction from hundreds to under ten suppliers, and claims about how often an internal team does or does not complete a task.
