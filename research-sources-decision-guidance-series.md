## Editorial decisions

The supplied drafts contain specific fee ranges, company-size thresholds, delivery timelines, staffing economics, grant-program examples, and claims about Enduring Advantage’s commercial structure. These claims are not independently sourced in the drafts. The published series should therefore use bounded, decision-led language rather than present these items as universal facts or current offers.
