# Planetary Boundaries Article Source Notes

## Primary sources retained in the public article

1. **Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK), 21 September 2026.** “Planetary Health Check 2026 finds mounting pressures across Earth’s life support systems.”
   - URL: https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/news/latest-news/planetary-health-check-2026-finds-mounting-pressures-across-earth2019s-life-support-systems
   - Confirms seven of nine Planetary Boundaries are transgressed; climate change, biosphere integrity, land-system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical flows, novel entities, and ocean acidification.
   - Confirms ocean acidification crossed its boundary in 2025 and that all seven breached boundaries are at their highest recorded levels of transgression.
   - Confirms vegetation and soils absorb a substantial share of human carbon dioxide emissions and that the report discusses possible overestimation of land-sink resilience.
   - Reports recent extremes including Pakistan’s 2025 floods, record ocean heat content, Hurricane Melissa, coral-bleaching heat stress, European drought and wildfires, and record heat in several regions.

2. **CDP, 12 May 2026.** “Extreme Weather Risk is Reshaping the Global Economy to the Tune of Over US$700 Billion in Projected Losses.”
   - URL: https://www.cdp.net/en/press-releases/extreme-weather-risk-is-reshaping-the-global-economy
   - Based on full environmental disclosures from 11,261 companies in 2025.
   - Confirms US$714 billion in anticipated future financial impacts from extreme weather, principally flooding, cyclones, and heavy rain.
   - Confirms 48 percent of identified risks are expected within two years.
   - Confirms a median US$39.4 million cost of risk per company versus US$3.1 million to mitigate it, described by CDP as nearly 13 times lower.
   - Notes the figure was corrected from an earlier US$898 billion estimate to US$714 billion after a dataset review.

3. **Planetary Boundaries Science Lab, Planetary Health Check 2026.**
   - URL: https://www.planetaryhealthcheck.org/
   - Confirms seven of nine boundaries are breached and describes the framework as scientifically defined guardrails for Earth-system resilience.
