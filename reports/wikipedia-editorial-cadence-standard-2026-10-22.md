# Wikipedia-Based Editorial Cadence Standard

**Purpose:** Apply a practical editorial standard to Enduring Advantage’s reserved website copy so that it reads as specific, credible and human rather than templated or AI-generated. This is a style guide for a financial-decision website, not an instruction to write the site like an encyclopaedia.

## Working rules

1. Lead with the concrete point. A heading and its first sentence should tell the reader what matters before providing method, background or qualification.
2. Use plain, precise words. Explain specialist terms at their first meaningful use. Keep necessary terms such as financial return, baseline and verification, but avoid internal shorthand or inflated language.
3. Keep one main idea in each sentence. Vary sentence length naturally, but do not use abrupt fragments merely to create drama.
4. Prefer active, attributable language. Name the actor when it matters. Remove vague authority phrases such as “experts say”, “it is believed” or “research has shown” unless the source is named.
5. Remove filler, duplication and ornamental transitions. Do not add a synonym simply to avoid repeating a useful word. Do not stack lists of abstract nouns.
6. Avoid the common AI vocabulary pattern unless the word is the most accurate option in context. In particular, review generic uses of: additionally, align with, bolster, crucial, delve, emphasize, enhance, foster, highlight, intricate, landscape, pivotal, robust, showcase, underscore, valuable, vibrant, leverage, multifaceted and practical.
7. Keep sales claims concrete and restrained. State the mechanism, the boundary and the decision the reader can make. Do not add unsupported superlatives, universal claims or vague proof.
8. Use contractions when they sound like an educated person speaking directly to a client. Do not force contractions into formal disclosures, labels, legal text, source descriptions or technical instructions.
9. Use Canadian spelling consistently. Retain the approved lower-case **actio** wordmark, and do not introduce em dashes.
10. Preserve approved facts, evidence, ownership disclosures, exact Energy Advantage data-capability wording, financial-return ranking, and the positive-impact gate across the entire Sustainability Playing Field.

## Sources consulted

Wikipedia’s clarity guidance says plain English works best and warns against jargon, “peacock terminology”, weasel words and vague attribution. Its writing guidance prioritizes a clear opening, concise paragraphs and precise terms. The descriptive Signs of AI writing page identifies patterns worth avoiding in this context, including dense clusters of generic AI vocabulary and unnatural lexical variation. The Signs page is descriptive rather than a standalone prescriptive style guide.
