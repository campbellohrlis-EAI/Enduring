# Homepage v4 evidence sources

## Verified claims for the proposed evidence section

- CDP's 12 May 2026 release states that 11,261 companies that disclosed full environmental data through CDP in 2025 anticipated US$714 billion in future financial impacts from extreme weather. It says US$326 billion is expected from reduced production capacity and 48% of identified risks are expected within two years. It also reports a median risk cost of US$39.4 million per company compared with US$3.1 million to mitigate, nearly 13 times lower.
  - Primary source: https://www.cdp.net/en/press-releases/extreme-weather-risk-is-reshaping-the-global-economy
  - Important wording note: CDP published the release in 2026 using 2025 disclosure data. The Homepage must not label the financial estimates as "CDP in 2025" or imply they are an individual-company forecast.

- The IEA's World Energy Investment 2025 executive summary estimates USD 2.2 trillion will go to renewables, nuclear, grids, storage, low-emissions fuels, efficiency and electrification in 2025, versus USD 1.1 trillion for oil, natural gas and coal. The IEA calls the values estimates.
  - Primary source: https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-investment-2025/executive-summary
  - Important wording note: the figure covers a defined energy-technology basket. It does not substantiate all sustainability products and services generally, and therefore should not be used to claim that all such products "pay for themselves."

## Application decision

Use the CDP figures with an on-page source line and present them as expectations from disclosing companies. Use the IEA investment figure only for the accurately scoped clean-energy-technology claim. Qualify the linkage to general sustainability innovation rather than claiming that investment proves all solutions pay for themselves.
