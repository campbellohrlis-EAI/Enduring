# Round 6 Research Claim Source Notes

These source notes support the limited public-copy corrections requested in the Round 6 audit.

| Claim area | Verified source | Usable wording |
|---|---|---|
| Non-financial data quality | [EY, 2024 Global Corporate Reporting Survey](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/insights/financial-accounting-advisory-services/corporate-reporting-survey) | EY reported that 96% of finance leaders identified some problem with the integrity of the non-financial data they receive for reporting. The survey was conducted for EY by FT Longitude between March and May 2024 and included more than 2,000 senior finance leaders. |
| Land degradation | [UNCCD, Global Land Outlook 2](https://www.unccd.int/resources/global-land-outlook/glo2) | UNCCD identifies Global Land Outlook 2 as its 2022 flagship report on reducing and reversing land degradation. The site should attribute any 40% global land-degradation language to UNCCD GLO2 and should not describe that figure as agricultural land. |
| Ocean acidification | [NOAA, Ocean acidification](https://www.noaa.gov/education/resource-collections/ocean-coasts/ocean-acidification) | NOAA reports that, in the 200-plus years since the Industrial Revolution began, surface-ocean pH has fallen by 0.1 pH units, approximately a 30% increase in acidity. NOAA also states that the ocean absorbs about 30% of carbon dioxide released to the atmosphere. |

The article updates should retain only claims that match these source notes or clearly identify a different named source.
